# Receipts That Can Be Checked

Engineering blog · JCEE WorldLab · 2026-11-05 · Version 1.0

Evidence export and reconstruction have advanced. A readable receipt still needs a supported conclusion and a clear trust boundary.

## A record should support examination

A receipt is useful when someone can examine what it says, what observations support it and what remains unresolved. A polished display alone does not establish that the underlying action occurred.

JCEE's recent work connects API and rendered evidence views with reconstruction checks. The original completed hosted actions were preserved rather than repeated to create a new demonstration.

## What the selected checks established

The November 4 component qualification checked a portable projection of nine retained evidence bundles: six from hosted work and three from local work. The selected validation rejected 27 altered inputs. Six confirmed native results also passed their original verifier.

The installed-interface evaluation checked agreement across clients and views, including invalid credentials, stale views and malformed or disconnected responses. These are results for the specific tested components and inputs.

## What a receipt cannot confer

Integrity checks do not make an untrustworthy source true. Evidence of an earlier result does not grant fresh permission, and a detected mismatch does not reverse an external effect.

The hosted evaluation used synthetic target effects. It did not make real provider actions or qualify the complete Evidence Engine as a production product. The full G0 integration gate remains unrun and release remains on hold.

## The next review

The version and acceptance mapping is prepared, and all 20 composite case recipes are frozen as a review candidate. Independent acceptance and final runtime bindings are required before execution.

The public record shares observable behavior and limitations. Detailed encodings, internal verification procedures and private evidence packages are not part of this publication.
