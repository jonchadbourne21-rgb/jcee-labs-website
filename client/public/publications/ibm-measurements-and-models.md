# IBM Measurements, Prediction Models and the Next Question

Research brief · JCEE WorldLab · 2026-11-05 · Version 1.0

A November update separating recorded quantum hardware observations, retrospective model analysis, mathematical results and a simulated follow-up.

## What the hardware record added

The BB84 program now includes measurements from IBM quantum hardware and a follow-up evaluation retaining 20,480 shots. The follow-up's primary comparisons did not detect the proposed effect after multiple-comparison correction. Non-detection is not proof of equivalence.

Separate Psi analogue and Reverse Terry work checked existing circuit files against authenticated provider records: nine jobs in the former record and eight in the latter. No new jobs were needed for those verification passes.

## A better prediction is not yet a mechanism

An offline audit checked the intended preparation and measurement against the executed circuits. The recorded calibration update did not explain the prediction gap. An initialization sensitivity model improved the description modestly, while an earlier fitted model remained more accurate on these data.

This is a retrospective analysis. Both frozen predictions and the original primary result remain unchanged. The analysis does not identify a unique cause or show that different hardware is necessary.

## A simulated follow-up

A classical simulation examines whether controlled circuit timing could distinguish stationary error from time-dependent behavior. Ideal-state, timing and numerical cross-checks passed, and the scientific outputs reproduced in a clean directory under the same operator.

Those outputs are synthetic predictions, not new hardware measurements. Fresh preflight, a frozen execution plan and explicit approval are required before any new run. The diagnostic would not by itself prove what caused the earlier gap.

## Keep the research strands distinct

Mathematical BB84 work has additional bounded certificates and exclusions, with general questions still open. The publicly released October 1 preprint is unchanged. Unpublished proofs and exact follow-up protocols remain private.

Discrete qubit observations do not establish living-cell entanglement, a new quantum algorithm, independent pulse-level attestation or certification. Biological proposals and third-party collaboration remain separate from these observations.
