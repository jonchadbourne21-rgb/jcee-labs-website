## Frozen direction

Apple-inspired restraint and product focus; Temporal-inspired technical storytelling; an original JCEE evidence thread as the visual signature. References inform principles, not copied assets or layouts.

1. Opening: “When software acts, follow the evidence.” One supporting explanation; Explore the demonstration / Discuss your workflow.
2. Signature: one evidence thread connecting source instruction, current permission, consequential action and inspectable record. Reuse the motif in later brand work.
3. Challenge: a prominent, explicitly illustrative business scenario. Explore changed permission, interruption and conflicting records. Never present browser animation as execution evidence.
4. Visual system: warm white business scenes; graphite technical scenes; cobalt accent; large sans-serif headings; readable body copy; monospace for evidence; purposeful motion.
5. Narrative: concrete business story before internal architecture. Preserve the distinctions among implemented reference software, recorded synthetic qualification, platform direction and production claims.
